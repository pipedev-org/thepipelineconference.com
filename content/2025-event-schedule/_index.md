## October 2025

**Thursday, October 28, 2025 from 9am - 10:30am **Pacific Time**  
Data Analytics at Image Engine: How Do We Measure "Normal"?**  
**[Register here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/data-analytics-at-image-engine-how-do-we-measure-normal-tickets-1740303708239?aff=TPCWebsite)**  
via Zoom  
Video will be recorded and put onto the [The Pipeline Conference youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/ThePipelineConference)

For nearly a decade [Image Engine](https://image-engine.com/) has been on a journey to become a more data assisted company. This presentation will cover that journey and discuss our implementation. We will discuss some of the pitfalls we faced and how we engaged the crew to help QC the data. How different data sources allow us to plan resources and make better decisions. We will also look at how we are expanding this process to the broader Cinesite group of companies.

**Speakers**  
**Greg Massie**  
As Head of CG at Image Engine, Greg Massie brings nearly 25 years of visual effects experience to his role overseeing cross-departmental workflows and guiding strategic technology decisions. He works closely with both creative and technical teams to help supervisors and project leads deliver world-class content efficiently.

**Executive Host**  
**Sarah Haynes – Director of Production, Production Management, Autodesk**  
Sarah Haynes brings a unique perspective to production management, combining extensive hands-on experience with strategic leadership expertise. Currently serving as Director of Product, Production Management at Autodesk, she leads product development for Flow Production Tracking, Flow Generative Scheduling, and RV.

## July 2025

**Tuesday, July 15 at 9am Pacific Time**  
**Unlocking Production Efficiency with LAIKA**  
**[Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9GU298xHvOw&t=3s)**

LAIKA Studios has successfully applied visual effects engineering principles to business transformation. In this session, Mike Romey shares how his Business Systems team developed tools that support every department with scalable, cloud-based solutions.

Their Federated API unifies over 25 SaaS platforms—including ShotGrid, Dynamics 365, Greenhouse, and UKG—into a single query language, simplifying cross-functional integrations and ETL pipelines.

The team also built a Federated Forms Framework using Python and React to auto-generate complex UIs from JSON, allowing rapid internal tool development without snowflake apps. These frameworks enable consistent, efficient delivery across the studio.

**Speaker**

**Michael Romey** is a seasoned technology leader with over 25 years of experience building scalable platforms and leading high impact engineering teams across the media and entertainment industry. He currently serves as Manager of Business Intelligence Software Development at LAIKA Studios, where he oversees cross functional systems that connect production, finance, HR, and more.

**Executive Host**  
**Aaron Thibault**

## May 2025

**Thursday, May 8 at 1pm Pacific Time**  
**Industry Crossroads: An Open Forum on the Future of Pipelines**  
This video was not recorded so the discussion could be as free and open as possible.

We're hosting a community round-table, and we'd love for you to join the conversation about production pipelines! We'll be sharing what we're seeing and thinking about how things are changing.

With the recent shifts in the industry and the buzz around AI, it feels like a really important time for us to connect and have an open dialogue. This is about getting together as a community and sharing our perspectives on what's happening.

Here's what we're hoping for:

- A straightforward discussion about where pipeline development stands today.
- An open exploration of new technologies and how they might fit in.
- A chance to put our heads together and think about sustainable ways forward.
- A great opportunity to connect with peers and swap experiences.
- A space for you to bring your questions, insights, and innovative ideas to the table!

We truly believe that by coming together and sharing our thoughts, we can navigate these changes and build a stronger future for our community.

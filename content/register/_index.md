## TPC 2026 is online, and free

Our in-person conference on 17 September 2026 was cancelled. The programme ran online instead, as two free half-days on Tuesday 20 and Tuesday 27 October, with the Pipeline Awards Show to follow in December. There was no ticket price and no travel.

Both half-days and the Speaker Series kickoff have happened, and the recordings are free to watch on YouTube. To hear about the Awards Show and everything after it, [subscribe to the newsletter](https://newsletter.pipedev.org/forms/nfrm_BzLMaLXv) or join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/PU8hygUfbf).
